Know which crafters you can trust. Crafting orders are full of crafters you know nothing about. Trust Crafter keeps a private ledger of every order you place, so the next time you see a crafter you can tell at a glance whether they've done good work for you before.

  • Every order logged automatically: crafter, quality asked for and received, time taken and tip.
  • Outcomes from the facts: Met, Exceeded or Below, never a star rating.
  • Right where you need it: tooltips, the crafting orders window and the right-click menu.
  • Online dot: see whether a crafter you trust is online right now.
  • Crafted for others: crafters get a log of every order they fill, with craft times and tips.
  • Works with CraftScan: your history with a customer, right in CraftScan's tooltips.
  • Guild sharing: see how your guild's orders with a crafter went, checked against the crafter's own log.
  • Realm sharing: ask other Trust Crafter users on your realm too, always shown apart as unverified.
  • New: Find a Crafter: see who on your realm can craft a recipe, sorted by their history and reviews.

No typing and no setup. Place orders as usual and Trust Crafter fills itself in.

Logs Every Order for You
Placing a crafting order logs it straight away. For each one Trust Crafter records who crafted it, the quality you asked for and the quality you got, when it was placed and filled, how long it took, the tip you paid and whether it was a public, guild or personal order. Collecting the crafted item from your mail marks it filled, with the crafter and quality read from the mail, so there's no need to go back to the crafting orders NPC. An order one of your own characters fills is marked the moment you fill it. Orders placed before you installed it are added too, for as long as the game still lists them.

Outcomes From the Facts
Each order is marked Met, Exceeded or Below by comparing the quality you asked for with the quality you got. Orders nobody filled are kept as Not filled and never counted against anyone. Results read as rates, not stars: "4/4 met or exceeded" tells you far more than a score.

Shows It Where You Need It

  • Tooltip: hover a player you've used as a crafter to see crafted 4x for you | 4/4 met or exceeded | avg 38 min.
  • Crafting orders window: type a crafter's name for a personal order and your history with them shows beside it. In My Orders, a tick marks crafters you've used before, yellow if any order came in below what you asked for.
  • Right-click menu on a player's name in chat, the friends list, your guild or a unit frame opens your history with that crafter.
  • New: when you craft: browsing orders as a crafter, a tick marks customers you've crafted for before, with how many times and their tips on hover and beside their name in the order.

Is the Crafter Online?
A dot beside each crafter: green when they're online, red when the game says they're offline, grey when it can't tell. It uses the game's own whisper check plus your friends list, group and Battle.net friends, and sends nothing to the crafter. Shown in the history window, the order details, the personal order name box, the My Orders tooltip and /tc lookup.

History Window and Export
Open it with /tc or the minimap button: every order on your realm, sortable by date, crafter, item, outcome, time taken or tip, with search and outcome and profession filters. /tc export or the Export button gives you every order as CSV for a spreadsheet, including the ones you crafted for others.

New: Crafted for Others
Crafting for other players? The second tab of the history window logs every order you fill: who it was for, the quality you delivered against what they asked for, your craft time from taking the order to filling it, and the tip. The top of the list adds up your orders, customers and tips. Hover a player you've crafted for and the tooltip says you crafted 3x for them | 45g in tips. Orders from your own characters and NPC (patron) orders aren't counted here.

New in 2.0: Guild Sharing You Can Check
Turn on Share with my guild in the options, and when you look a crafter up, Trust Crafter asks your guild how their orders with them went, even for a crafter you've never used: Guild: 7 orders from 3 people | 6/7 met or exceeded | 5 confirmed. Answers come straight from whoever wrote them, and nothing is passed on.

If the crafter runs Trust Crafter too, their own log of the orders they filled is asked about each one:

  • Confirmed: the crafter's log says the same quality.
  • Disputed: the crafter's log says a different quality.
  • No record: the crafter has no record of that order.

Shared: outcome, quality asked for and received, time taken, order type, and tags and notes not marked private. Never tips, never outside your guild. Off until you turn it on, and it pauses during raid encounters and Mythic+ runs. A fake bad review against a crafter who runs Trust Crafter shows up as no record, so honest crafters have every reason to install it.

New in 4.0: Crafter Desk
Turn it on and Trust Crafter watches Trade, Services and your whispers for people asking for something one of your characters can craft. Each alert shows who's asking and what for, which of your characters makes it, the quality you can reach with and without concentration, roughly what the materials cost (with Auction Coach installed), your history with them, and how often it was asked for today.

  • Greet whispers a reply in your tone (Friendly, Short or Formal), built from varied pieces so it never reads like a bot. It answers what they asked: quality, whose mats, your commission and which character to send the order to. Hover to read it first, right-click for another wording.
  • Catches the way people really ask: "LF generosity chest" finds your Allied Chestplate of Generosity, without you typing a single keyword.
  • All your crafters in one place: tick which characters to watch for, and give each a watchlist with the quality it makes, your own keywords per item and Only max quality. When several of your characters know a recipe, the one that makes it at max quality answers.
  • Write your own replies per character with placeholders like {name}, {item}, {character}, {quality} and {commission}, or use the built-in ones.
  • A small desk button that stays out of the way: put it below the minimap, at the top, on the right edge, above the action bars or anywhere you drag it, or turn it off. It lights up when someone asks; hover to read what you'd say, click to greet, middle-click to whisper, right-click to dismiss. Key bindings too.
  • Never automatic: one click per whisper, one greeting per request, at most 5 a minute.
  • /tc desk lists every request from the last week with how many you greeted and how many ordered, plus settings: which chats, your tone and commission, the request and ignore words, and a box to test any chat line.
  • Right-click a name in chat for Reply as a crafter. Off until you turn it on.

New in 3.1: Find a Crafter
Placing a personal order and don't know who to send it to? Click Find a Crafter beside the name box. Trust Crafter asks other Trust Crafter users on your realm, and your guild, who knows the recipe, and lists them with your history and reviews: crafters you've had good orders from first, then good guild and realm reviews, then whoever is online and not busy. Use puts their name on the order, Whisper opens a whisper, and hovering shows their full history.

  • Crafters: turn on Find a Crafter and open each profession once, and your characters are listed when someone searches for a recipe you know. Your alts can be listed too, or turn being listed off.
  • Busy right now? /tc busy keeps you listed, marked busy. Add a short note customers see with /tc note, like Free with your mats.
  • Answers come straight from the crafter by whisper, on the same hidden channel as realm sharing. Off until you turn it on.

Realm Sharing
Turn on Share on my realm in the options and Trust Crafter also asks other Trust Crafter users on your realm about a crafter, on a hidden channel you never see in chat. Their answers are always shown on their own line, Realm (unverified): 4 orders from 2 people | 4/4 met or exceeded, never mixed into what you and your guild know, and they're checked against the crafter's own log just like guild answers. It's separate from guild sharing, off by default, and notes marked private never leave your computer.

  • Counted by people, not orders, with limits per person so nobody can flood a crafter's page.
  • Don't trust someone's reviews? Right-click them and choose Ignore their Trust Crafter reviews.
  • Realm answers are forgotten after 60 days, and you can delete them all at any time.
  • The channel takes one of your chat channel slots; /tc realm says whether it's connected.

Works With CraftScan
Use CraftScan to find customers in chat? Hover a customer in its orders window or its new request banner and Trust Crafter adds your history with them under the chat: you crafted 3x for them | 150g in tips. You know straight away who you've crafted for before and who tips well, before you answer. Nothing changes if CraftScan isn't installed.

Notes and Tags
Add a short note and tags like Fast, Friendly, Own mats, Would again or Slow to your own orders. Your words sit next to the facts, never on top of them.

Fair to Crafters

  • Only the character who placed an order can rate it.
  • Orders crafted by one of your own characters can't be rated, so nobody rates their own work, not even on an alt.
  • The facts can't be edited, and there's no "failed" button: an order that met the quality you asked for always shows as met.
  • How long an order took is shown as a number, never as a score. They might have been asleep.

Commands

  • /tc: open or close your order history (or click the minimap button)
  • /tc lookup Name: what you know about a crafter (no name: your target)
  • /tc export: copy your orders as CSV
  • /tc tooltip: turn the tooltip line on or off
  • /tc options: the options page (also in Esc > Options > AddOns)
  • /tc minimap: hide or show the minimap button
  • /tc wipe: delete your history on this realm (asks first)
  • /tc realm: whether realm sharing is connected
  • /tc ignore Name-Realm: ignore a player's realm reviews (/tc unignore to undo)
  • /tc busy: mark yourself busy (or not) for Find a Crafter
  • /tc note text: a short note customers see when they find you
  • /tc help: every command
