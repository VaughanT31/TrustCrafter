Know which crafters you can trust

Crafting orders are full of crafters you know nothing about. TrustCrafter keeps a private ledger of every order you place, so next time you can see at a glance whether a crafter has done good work for you before.

What it does

  • Logs every order automatically: who crafted it, the quality you asked for and got, when it was placed and filled, how long it took, and the tip you paid. No typing needed.
  • Outcome from the facts: each order is marked Met, Exceeded or Below by comparing what you asked for with what you got. Orders nobody filled are kept, but never held against anyone.
  • Picked up when you collect your mail: no need to go back to the crafting orders NPC.
  • Your notes and tags (Fast, Friendly, Own mats, Would again, Slow) sit next to the facts, never on top of them.
  • Everywhere you need it: hover a crafter, right-click their name in chat, or type it for a personal order to see crafted 4x for you | 4/4 met or exceeded | avg 38 min.
  • Online dot beside each crafter: green online, red offline, grey when the game can't tell.
  • Export your orders as CSV for a spreadsheet.
  • History window (/tc): every order on your realm, sortable, with search and outcome and profession filters.

Fair to crafters

  • You can only rate a crafter you actually placed an order with.
  • The facts can't be edited, and there is no "failed" button: an order that met the quality you asked for always shows as met.
  • How long an order took is shown as a number, never as a score.
  • Nobody can rate their own work, not even on an alt.

Commands

  • /tc or the minimap button opens your order history
  • /tc lookup Name shows what you know about a crafter
  • /tc help lists all commands
