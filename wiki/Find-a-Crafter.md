# Find a Crafter

*Ask your realm and guild who can craft a recipe, sorted by trust.*

Turn on **Find a Crafter** in the options, or click the **Find a Crafter** button on a personal order (it asks first).

The button asks your guild and realm who knows the recipe. Crafters with Find a Crafter on answer with which of their characters know it.

Only crafters your order can reach are listed: characters on your realm or a realm connected to it. Guildmates on other realms are left out, since a personal order can't be sent to them.

### Results

Sorted by trust: your own good orders first, then good guild reviews, then good realm reviews, then crafters who are online and not busy. Your own characters come last.

- **Use** puts the name in the order's recipient box.
- **Whisper** opens a whisper.
- Hover a row for the full history.

### As a crafter

- Open each profession once per character, so its recipes are known.
- **Include my other characters** lists your alts too. **Let customers find me** can be turned off to search without being listed.
- `/tc busy` marks you busy. `/tc note Free with your mats` adds a short note customers see.
