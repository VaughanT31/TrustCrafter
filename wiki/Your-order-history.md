# Your order history

*Every order you placed, its outcome, and your own notes and tags.*

Open it with `/tc` or the minimap button. It lists every order on this realm, sortable by date, crafter, item, outcome, time taken or tip, with search and filters for outcome and profession.

![The history window](https://ctrlshiftzed.com/assets/img/shots/tc-history.webp)
*The history window*

### For each order

- **Who crafted it.**
- **The quality you asked for and the quality you got.**
- **When it was placed and filled**, and how long that took.
- **The tip** and whether it was a public, guild or personal order.

### Outcomes

Each order is marked by comparing the quality you asked for with what you got:

| Outcome | Means |
|---|---|
| **Exceeded** | Better than you asked for |
| **Met** | What you asked for |
| **Below** | Worse than you asked for |

Orders that were never filled are kept, but never held against anyone.

### Your notes and tags

Add a note and tags (**Fast**, **Friendly**, **Own mats**, **Would again**, **Slow**). Your words sit next to the facts, never on top of them:

- You can only add a note to an order you placed, on the character that placed it.
- The facts can't be edited, and there's no "failed" button.
- Orders crafted by one of your own characters can't be rated, so nobody reviews their own work.

> Mail doesn't say exactly when it was sent, so a time worked out from mail shows with a "~".
