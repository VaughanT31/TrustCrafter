# TrustCrafter wiki

How to use TrustCrafter: your private order history, seeing it where you need it, sharing with your guild and realm, finding a crafter and the Crafter Desk for crafters.

Also on the website: [TrustCrafter wiki](https://ctrlshiftzed.com/wiki/trustcrafter/) | Get it on [CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/trust-crafter)

## Pages

- [Getting started](Getting-started.md): Install it and place orders as usual: TrustCrafter logs them by itself.
- [Your order history](Your-order-history.md): Every order you placed, its outcome, and your own notes and tags.
- [Where you see it](Where-you-see-it.md): Tooltips, the crafting orders window, right-click menus and online dots.
- [Crafting for others](Crafting-for-others.md): Your side of the orders you fill, and what you know about your customers.
- [Guild sharing](Guild-sharing.md): Ask your guild how their orders with a crafter went, even one you've never used.
- [Realm sharing](Realm-sharing.md): Ask other TrustCrafter users on your realm about a crafter.
- [Find a Crafter](Find-a-Crafter.md): Ask your realm and guild who can craft a recipe, sorted by trust.
- [Crafter Desk](Crafter-Desk.md): For crafters: an alert when someone in chat asks for something you can craft.
- [Export and options](Export-and-options.md): Copy your orders to a spreadsheet, and what each option does.
- [Commands](Commands.md): Every TrustCrafter chat command.
- [Questions and fixes](Questions-and-fixes.md): Answers to the things people ask most.
