# Where you see it

*Tooltips, the crafting orders window, right-click menus and online dots.*

### Player tooltips

Hover a player you've used as a crafter:

`TrustCrafter: crafted 4x for you | 4/4 met or exceeded | avg 38 min`

### The crafting orders window

- Type a crafter's name for a **personal order** and your history with them shows beside the name.
- In **My Orders**, a tick marks crafters you've used before (yellow if an order came in below what you asked for). Hover the order for your history.

### Right-click menus

Right-click a player's name in chat, your friends list, your guild or a unit frame. The TrustCrafter line opens your history with that crafter.

### Is the crafter online?

A dot beside each crafter: **green** online, **red** offline, **grey** when the game can't tell (for example a player on another realm). It uses the game's own checks plus your friends list and group, and sends nothing to the crafter.
