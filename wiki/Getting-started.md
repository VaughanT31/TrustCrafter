# Getting started

*Install it and place orders as usual: TrustCrafter logs them by itself.*

TrustCrafter remembers who crafted your orders, what quality you asked for and what you got. Next time you see a crafter, you can tell at a glance whether they've done good work for you before.

### Install

1. **CurseForge app:** search for **TrustCrafter** and click Install.
2. **By hand:** download it from [CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/trust-crafter) and put the **TrustCrafter** folder in `World of Warcraft\_retail_\Interface\AddOns`.
3. Start the game, or type `/reload`.

### How orders get logged

There's nothing to type:

1. **Place an order** at a crafting orders table. It's logged straight away.
2. **Collect your mail.** The crafted item arrives by mail, and TrustCrafter reads the crafter and quality from it.
3. **Open My Orders** at the table any time; that updates everything the game lists there too.

Orders you placed before installing are added as well, for as long as the game still lists them.

### See your history

Type `/tc` or click the minimap button. See [Your order history](Your-order-history.md).

Sharing with your guild or realm, Find a Crafter and the Crafter Desk are all **off** until you turn them on.
