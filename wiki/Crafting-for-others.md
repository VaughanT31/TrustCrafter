# Crafting for others

*Your side of the orders you fill, and what you know about your customers.*

### Crafted for others

The second tab of the history window logs every order you fill for another player: who it was for, the quality you delivered against what they asked, how long you took, and the tip. The top adds up how many orders, for how many customers and how much in tips.

Orders from your own characters stay on My orders, and NPC (patron) orders aren't logged.

### Your customers

Browsing public, guild or personal orders as a crafter, a tick marks customers you've crafted for before. Hover the order for how many times and how much they tipped.

Hover a player you've crafted for and the tooltip says so too ("you crafted 3x for them").

### Works with CraftScan

If you use CraftScan to find customers in chat, hovering a customer in its window shows your history with them. Nothing changes if CraftScan isn't installed.
