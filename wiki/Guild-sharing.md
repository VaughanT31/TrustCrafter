# Guild sharing

*Ask your guild how their orders with a crafter went, even one you've never used.*

Turn on **Share with my guild** in the options (Esc > Options > AddOns > TrustCrafter, or `/tc options`).

Then look a crafter up (history window, `/tc lookup Name`, the personal order name box, or a My Orders row) and TrustCrafter asks your guild:

`Guild: 7 orders from 3 people | 6/7 met or exceeded | 5 confirmed`

### Confirmed orders

If the crafter runs TrustCrafter and is online, their own Crafted for others log is checked against those orders: **confirmed** (same quality), **disputed** (a different quality) or **no record**.

### What's shared, and what isn't

- **Shared:** outcome, quality asked for and received, time taken, order type, and tags and notes not marked private.
- **Never shared:** tips, or anything outside your guild.
- Answers come straight from whoever wrote them. The game fills in who sent each message, so nobody can answer as someone else.
- *Confirm orders I crafted* only tells a customer the quality you delivered for their order, never who an order was for.
