# Realm sharing

*Ask other TrustCrafter users on your realm about a crafter.*

Turn on **Share on my realm** in the options. TrustCrafter then also asks other TrustCrafter users on your realm about a crafter.

- Realm answers are shown on their own as **Realm (unverified)**, never mixed into yours or your guild's.
- At most 20 orders per person per crafter, and 5 from the last week, counted by people rather than orders.
- Ignore a reviewer by right-clicking them, or with `/tc ignore Name-Realm` (`/tc unignore` to undo).
- Realm answers are forgotten after 60 days. **Forget realm reviews** in the options deletes them all.

### The hidden channel

Questions go on a hidden channel that's joined a minute after login and removed from every chat tab. It takes one of your 10 chat channel slots. `/tc realm` shows whether it's connected. Answers come back by whisper, so nothing is passed on by anyone else.
