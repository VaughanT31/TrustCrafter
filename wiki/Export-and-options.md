# Export and options

*Copy your orders to a spreadsheet, and what each option does.*

### Export

`/tc export`, or Export in the history window, gives you every order as CSV to paste into a spreadsheet: the ones you placed and the ones you crafted (the `side` column says which).

### Options

Esc > Options > AddOns > TrustCrafter, or `/tc options`:

- The tooltip line, online dots, minimap button, crafting orders window lines and CraftScan lines.
- Guild sharing, realm sharing, Find a Crafter and the Crafter Desk.
- Export, forget realm reviews, or delete your history.

Everything is kept per realm, because public crafting orders only reach crafters on your own realm.
