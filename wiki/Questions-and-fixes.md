# Questions and fixes

*Answers to the things people ask most.*

**An order isn't marked filled.**

Collect the mail it came in, or open My Orders at the crafting orders table. Either updates it.

**Why does the time taken show a "~"?**

It was worked out from the mail, which doesn't say exactly when it was sent.

**Why can't I add a note to an order?**

Only the character that placed an order can rate it, and orders crafted by your own characters can't be rated.

**Can a crafter see what I wrote about them?**

Only if you turn on guild or realm sharing, and only notes you haven't marked private. Tips are never shared.

**I lost a chat channel slot.**

Realm sharing and Find a Crafter use one hidden channel, which takes one of your 10 slots. Turn both off and it's freed.

**Is my history on other realms?**

History is kept per realm, because public orders only reach crafters on your own realm.

**Does the Crafter Desk whisper people by itself?**

Never. Every whisper is one click from you.
